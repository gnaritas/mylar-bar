The story so far

A party store, a party, and a bar named after balloon film

Liv Arterbridge and Gina Piccari — two South Philly hospitality veterans who met in 2016 — bought the old Dino’s Party Center building in October 2024. What opened in November 2026 is Mylar Bar: named for the shiny film party balloons are made of, built for the neighborhood that bought them.
