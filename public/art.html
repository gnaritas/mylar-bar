The Walls

The art rotates.
The party doesn’t.

Mylar Bar hangs rotating work from neighborhood artists, tied to Blah Blah Gallery in Bella Vista. Walk in twice in a row and you’ll see two different shows.

Plan your visit Artists: get in touch

Tied to Blah Blah Gallery

The rotation runs through Blah Blah Gallery, the artist-run space in nearby Bella Vista — so the work on Mylar’s walls comes from the scene that lives around the bar, not from a stock-art crate.

Why it fits

A room that started life as a party store deserves changing walls. Balloons and streamers used to cover these walls; now it’s paintings and prints from the neighborhood — same idea, better company.

Painting? Making something?

The bar wants local work on the walls. Send us a note through the contact form — include a couple of links and the gallery crew will see it.
