Our Story

From party store
to party.

Mylar Bar took over the South Philadelphia building where Dino’s Party Center sold balloons, decorations and party supplies for decades — before Dino’s itself moved across the street. The Inquirer called what rose in its place “a festive neighborhood bar”. We like that description.

2016

Two cooks, one kitchen

Gina Piccari hired Liv Arterbridge into the kitchen at Boot & Saddle. The two South Philadelphians have worked hospitality side by side ever since.

Oct 2024

The building

Liv and Gina bought the former Dino’s Party Center at Ninth and Morris — the hall where the neighborhood bought balloons and party supplies for decades, right up until Dino’s moved across the street.

2026

Almost in summer

When fall opened, the Inquirer’s food writers already had Mylar Bar on their list of new Philadelphia restaurants to watch. The doors took a little longer than planned — worth the wait.

Nov 2026

Open

Mylar Bar opened in mid-November 2026, after a friends-and-family weekend. Now: open every day, 5 pm to 2 am, kitchen until 1.

The name

“Mylar” — the shiny film party balloons are made of.

the bar is named for the material Dino’s built a business on

The kitchen is led by chef Colin White. The drink list leans martinis and punches. The art changes every visit. Everything else — the neighborhood feeling — came free with the building.
