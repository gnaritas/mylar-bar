What we collect

The contact form on this site collects only the information you type into it — your name, your email address and your message — and sends it to the shop’s own email address so the owners can answer you. That is the entire purpose of the data. It is not sold, shared or used for advertising.

This site sets no cookies and uses no advertising trackers or analytics pixels of its own. The embedded Google map may set its own cookies once you interact with it, governed by Google’s privacy policy.
